# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Anura welcoming and respectful for
everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or level of
contribution. We expect all participants to act professionally and constructively.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, implementation and evidence, not people.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding publication of another person's personal
  information without permission.
- Taking responsibility, apologising and learning when mistakes occur.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, intimidation, personal attacks,
discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, unwanted publication of private
information and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider
inappropriate in a professional project community.

## Scope

This Code applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews and project-related communication, as well as
representations of the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code.
They may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, or
restrict participation when necessary and proportionate.

To report a conduct concern privately, contact the project maintainer through
the [D3M-Sudo GitHub profile](https://github.com/D3M-Sudo). Do not use public
issues for sensitive reports. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
reasonably possible.
